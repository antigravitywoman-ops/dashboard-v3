# MANUAL Workflow: Citation Building

This is a human-in-the-loop workflow. The AI identifies missing directory listings, but a human (or a specialized tool like Yext/BrightLocal) must execute the submissions to navigate CAPTCHAs, account validations, and unique website formats.

---

## Step 1 — Load Setup Data

**Actor**: `SEO Lead (Human)`
**Source**: `companies/<slug>/about/profile.md`

1. Ensure the core NAP data is definitively filled out in the profile:
    - **Exact Business Name**
    - **Physical Address** (must match Google Business Profile exactly)
    - **Local Phone Number**
    - **Website URL**
2. Consistency is critical. Using variations like "Ave" vs "Avenue" can dilute citation power.

---

## Step 2 — Review Target Directories

**Actor**: `SEO Lead (Human)`
**Source**: `companies/<slug>/memory/sheets/07-citations-backlinks.md` (Section A — Citations Checklist)

1. Open the weekly Citations Checklist generated by the `research-analyst`.
2. This sheet ranks missing directories by domain authority (e.g., Yelp, YellowPages, Bing Places, Apple Maps, industry-specific portals).

---

## Step 3 — Execute Registrations

**Actor**: `SEO Lead (Human)` OR `Data Entry Specialist`

1. Navigate to the target directories.
2. Create accounts using a dedicated company email (e.g., `listings@brand.com`).
3. Submit the exact NAP profile, business category, business hours, a brief description, and the company logo.
4. Complete any phone or email verification steps required by the directory.

---

## Step 4 — Log Completions

**Actor**: `SEO Lead (Human)`
**Target**: `companies/<slug>/memory/sheets/07-citations-backlinks.md`

1. Update the checklist, changing the status of submitted directories from `[Pending]` to `[Submitted]` or `[Live]`.
2. Add the public profile URL to the sheet for tracking.
3. In the next weekly audit cycle, the `research-analyst` will read the updated checklist and provide the next batch of citation targets.
