# MANUAL Workflow: Outreach Execution

This is a human-in-the-loop workflow. Automated agents identify targets and draft the emails, but a human must execute the final transmission to ensure quality, personalization, and deliverability.

---

## Step 0 — Phase Gate

**Phase requirement**: Link building outreach is a **Scale-phase task only**. Executing outreach before the site has sufficient on-page content, technical health, and live published content yields negligible results and wastes relationship capital.

**Actor**:  — verify this before executing any outreach.

Check  and read :

|  | Action |
|---|---|
| Foundation | **STOP.** Do not execute outreach. Return to technical and on-page tasks. |
| Growth | **STOP** unless  is also present in the plan frontmatter. If not met, defer. |
| Scale | **PROCEED** to Step 1. |
| Optimization | **PROCEED** to Step 1. |

If phase is Foundation or Growth:
- Do not open any outreach draft files
- Log the blocked attempt to : 
- If this block has occurred for 2+ consecutive weeks, notify operator via a  task that Scale unlock conditions should be reviewed

---

## Step 1 — Review the Pipeline

**Actor**: `SEO Lead (Human)`
**Source**: `companies/<slug>/memory/sheets/07-citations-backlinks.md` (Section B — Backlink Pipeline)

1. Open the Backlink Pipeline section from Sheet 07 (Citations & Backlinks) generated by the `research-analyst` during the weekly strategy cycle.
2. Review the list of target domains, DR (Domain Rating), and the identified contact URls.
3. Discard any targets that look spammy or irrelevant to the brand's industry.

---

## Step 2 — Fetch Outreach Drafts

**Actor**: `SEO Lead (Human)`
**Source**: `companies/<slug>/content/outreach-drafts/`

1. Navigate to the outreach drafts directory.
2. The `outreach-drafter` skill will have placed `.txt` or `.md` files here containing the pitch emails, personalized based on the target domain and the asset being promoted.
3. Review each draft for:
    - Tone and brand alignment.
    - Accurate inclusion of the `[Target Asset URL]`.
    - Natural phrasing (avoiding AI-sounding jargon).

---

## Step 3 — Execute Transmission

**Actor**: `SEO Lead (Human)`
**Tools**: Gmail, Outlook, Pitchbox, BuzzStream, or Hunter.io

1. Use a standard email client or an outreach platform to send the emails.
2. **Important**: Send from an authenticated inbox (Google Workspace/O365) assigned to the brand (e.g., `marketing@brand.com`) to avoid spam filters. Do not send from generic free webmail accounts.
3. Set a follow-up reminder for 3-5 days if using a standard email client, or configure the automated sequence in a tool like Pitchbox.

---

## Step 4 — Log Results

**Actor**: `SEO Lead (Human)`
**Target**: `companies/<slug>/technical/backlinks/acquired.md`

1. When a backlink is successfully acquired, log the live URL, the anchor text, and the date in the `acquired.md` file.
2. This allows the `research-analyst` to read the log in future cycles and understand which outreach angles are actually yielding results, creating a feedback loop for the AI.
