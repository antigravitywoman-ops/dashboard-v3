# Core Search Algorithm Updates & Agent Directives

This file tracks known search engine behavior shifts and the agent-level rules they impose. All agents must treat these as overriding directives when they conflict with default behavior.

---

## March 2024 Core Update (Google)

**Change**: Crackdown on scaled content abuse, programmatic content without added value, and site reputation abuse (parasite SEO).
**Impact**: Sites using templated content where pages differ only in a few swapped terms (like city names) saw significant ranking drops.

**Agent Rules**:
- All programmatic pages (e.g., location pages) MUST have unique, localized value-add content — not just a swapped city name. Minimum unique content per location page: 200 words of genuinely location-specific content (local stats, testimonials, case studies from that area, nearby landmarks).
- Any content generated by `blog-generate` must have a specific editorial angle — not generic topic coverage.
- The `research-analyst` must check Sheet 06 (Location Pages) "Content Strategy" column to ensure each entry specifies what makes that page unique.

---

## August 2024 Core Update (Google)

**Change**: Major emphasis on helpful content signals. Sites with content written primarily for search engines rather than users saw widespread demotion.
**Impact**: Thin affiliate pages, listicles without original analysis, and content with excessive keyword stuffing lost significant rankings.

**Agent Rules**:
- `blog-generate` must structure every post with clear first-hand perspective, specific examples, or original data. Generic information regurgitation is insufficient.
- Every blog post must answer the following implicitly: "Why is THIS company the right source for this information?" (E-E-A-T)
- Keyword density: target 1-2% for the primary keyword. Never deliberately repeat a keyword to hit a target count.

## March 2026 AI Overviews Expansion

**Change**: Google AI Overviews now appear for a significantly broader range of commercial and informational queries, including long-tail keywords previously showing only 10 blue links.
**Impact**: CTR for position 1-3 organic results is being canibalized for queries where AI Overviews appear. Informational queries are most affected. FAQ-style content with clear structured answers is being extracted into AI Overviews.

**Agent Rules**:
- `blog-generate` must structure content to be easily extractable: use ordered lists with concise paragraph explanations (not just bullet points), define technical terms clearly, use specific numbers where available.
- Always use `FAQPage` schema where 2+ natural Q&A sections exist in a piece of content.
- Prioritize building entity authority (brand mentions, citations, external links) as a hedge against AI Overview CTR loss.
- In Sheet 01 (Executive Summary), include "AI Overview Exposure" as a metric — estimate what % of the tracked keywords are showing AI Overviews in SERP.

## Local / Maps Relevance Signals (Ongoing)

**Change**: Google Local Pack ranking signals continue to emphasize behavioral signals (photo views, Q&A interactions, post engagement) alongside traditional NAP consistency and proximity.
**Impact**: GBP profiles with recent photo uploads, active Q&A, and weekly posts consistently outperform stale profiles even when NAP consistency is strong.

**Agent Rules**:
- `gbp-optimizer` weekly photo task must upload 2-3 fresh photos stamped with EXIF GPS coordinates matching the operating address.
- GBP posts must publish at least once per week — use the `gbp-optimizer` post action in the weekly tasks.
- Q&A sections with unanswered questions are a negative signal — run `gbp-optimizer` with `--action=qa` to respond to open questions.
- Monitor GBP photo view counts in the weekly snapshot for hospitality and local service clients — a drop in photo views is an early signal of local pack rank loss.

---

## E-E-A-T Signals (Ongoing — Post-2023)

**Change**: Google's quality rater guidelines emphasize Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trust (E-E-A-T) as key quality signals, particularly for YMYL (Your Money, Your Life) topics and B2B decision-making content.
**Impact**: Content without named authors, professional credentials, or verifiable expertise underperforms in YMYL categories.

**Agent Rules**:
- For B2B and professional service clients, `blog-generate` must include author attribution with credentials in every post. If no author bio exists, flag as a gap in the technical audit.
- Gap Analysis (Sheet 02) must include at least 5 E-E-A-T specific gaps per the sheet-metrics definition.
- Schema implementation must include `Person` schema for author bios and `Organization` schema with `sameAs` links to LinkedIn, Wikipedia, and other verifiable profiles.
