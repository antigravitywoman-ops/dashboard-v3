## 3. Language Guidelines

- **Sentence structure**: Short to medium. Conversational rhythm. Fragments are fine for emphasis.
- **Voice**: First-person, active. "I found that…" not "It has been found that…"
- **Reading level**: Young adult to early professional. Assume intelligence; avoid jargon.
- **Metaphors**: Use sparingly and grounded in everyday Indian life — not corporate/Western clichés.
- **Numbers**: Specific when impactful ("20 million page views", "123 Goodreads ratings", "4.69/5 stars").
- **Emotion**: Name it directly. "I was terrified." Not "It was a challenging period."
