## Executive Summary
Arpit Sharma is an Indian author, writer, and mentor based in Bhopal. He began publishing at 17 and has since accumulated 20M+ page views across his writing career, including 5M+ from his tenure as Junior Editor at TelecomTalk. He is a published author with two books — a young adult novel and a self-help title — and runs the Stoics Mentorship Program for UPSC Civil Services 2027 aspirants. The site serves as his central portfolio: showcasing his books, blog content on personal development and writing craft, and his mentorship program.
