# Audience Profiles — Arpit Sharma Writing

## Persona 1: The Overwhelmed Achiever (Primary)

**Name**: Priya, 26, Pune
**Occupation**: Software engineer at a mid-size IT firm
**Situation**: High-performer at work but privately struggling with purpose, burnout, and relationships. Reads self-help books late at night. Has a growing pile of half-finished journals.
**Goals**: Inner peace, clarity about life direction, healthier relationships with ambition and failure
**Pain points**: Generic self-help feels performative. Tired of "5 AM club" content. Wants something honest that doesn't pretend life is simple.
**Why Arpit**: *The Answers Within* is raw and relatable. He doesn't pretend to have all the answers — and that honesty is exactly what she's been looking for.
**How she finds the site**: Goodreads, Amazon book search, Instagram reels, Medium recommendations
