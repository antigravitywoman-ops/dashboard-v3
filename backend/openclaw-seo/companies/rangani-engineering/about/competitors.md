# Initial Competitor Baseline

From our footprint analysis on IndiaMart, TradeIndia, and generic SERPs for baling presses, primary competitors in the Indian heavy machinery and scrap baling space include:

1.  **Advance Machinery Corporation**: Strong presence in hydraulic baling presses.
2.  **Kaps Engineers**: Specializes in shredders and balers.
3.  **Hydrotek Engineers**: Direct competitor in hydraulic press manufacturing in Gujarat.
4.  **Amshtech**: Competes heavily in the scrap baling and shearing machine segment.
5.  **MacBaler**: International competitor offering similar ranges.
