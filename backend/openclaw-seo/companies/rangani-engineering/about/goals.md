# Primary Business Goals

## 1. Grow Domestic Heavy Machinery Market Share
Increase visibility and inbound lead generation for flagship products (Triple Action Hydraulic Scrap Baling Presses, 800-ton hydraulic presses) among large-scale Indian steel plants and recycling facilities.

## 2. Expand Export Footprint
Capitalize on existing footholds in Kazakhstan, Myanmar, Mauritius, Zambia, and Qatar by driving highly targeted, location-specific organic traffic for key machinery terms in these export markets.

## 3. Elevate Brand Authority
Transition digital perception from a standard supplier to an industry-leading, ISO 9001:2008 certified manufacturer with three decades of engineering expertise. 

## 4. Unify the Digital Ecosystem
Consolidate disjointed web presence (multiple domains like ranganiindia.com and ranganiscrapbailer.com) into a cohesive, canonical digital strategy that funnels B2B procurement professionals effectively.
