# Target Audience — Rangani Engineering Pvt. Ltd.

## Persona 1: The Indian Plant Manager (Primary)

**Role**: Plant Manager, Production Head, or Works Manager at a steel plant, metal recycling facility, or industrial manufacturing unit
**Situation**: Needs to procure or upgrade heavy industrial equipment — hydraulic presses, baling machines, scrap processing lines. Has budget authority or influences the decision. Operates under pressure to reduce downtime and improve throughput.
**Goals**: Reliable machinery with consistent performance, clear technical specifications, competitive pricing, fast delivery, and accessible after-sales support in Gujarat
**Pain Points**: Machine downtime affecting production targets, difficulty comparing supplier specs, long lead times, unclear warranty terms, limited local service support
**Decision Triggers**: Equipment failure, capacity expansion, new regulatory requirements (e.g., scrap management mandates), competitive pricing from new suppliers
**Discovery Path**: Google search for machinery terms, IndiaMart enquiries, trade show visits, peer referrals within the Gujarat/Rajasthan industrial community, distributor recommendations

---
## Persona 2: The Export Procurement Officer (Secondary)

**Role**: Procurement Manager or Technical Buyer at an industrial facility in Kazakhstan, Myanmar, Mauritius, Zambia, or Qatar
**Situation**: Sourcing industrial equipment for new plant setup or capacity expansion. Needs equipment that meets international quality standards, ships with documentation, and comes with installation support.
**Goals**: Competitive CIF/FOB pricing, documentation package (test certificates, warranty), reliable shipping coordination, English-speaking support
**Pain Points**: Language barriers, unclear import documentation, trust in Indian manufacturing quality, long delivery lead times from India
**Decision Triggers**: New government infrastructure projects requiring scrap processing equipment, plant modernization mandates, peer recommendations from existing export clients
**Discovery Path**: IndiaMART, trade directories, Google ("hydraulic press India manufacturer"), trade fair visits, referral from existing Rangani export clients

---
## Persona 3: The Scrap Yard Owner (Tertiary)

**Role**: Owner or operator of a metal scrap yard, junkyard, or car recycling facility
**Situation**: Needs to process scrap efficiently to maximize bale weight and transport efficiency. Working with tight margins — equipment cost must justify throughput gains.
**Goals**: Maximise throughput, reduce labour costs, produce transport-ready bales, minimise machine downtime during peak scrap season
**Pain Points**: Unreliable equipment causing production losses, difficulty finding spare parts, machines that can't handle mixed metal grades, no on-site installation support
**Why Rangani**: Proven track record with Indian steel plants, range from 50-ton to 200-ton presses covers the full scrap yard spectrum, Rajkot base ensures spare parts availability
**Discovery Path**: IndiaMART, Google ("scrap baling press Gujarat"), referrals from other scrap yard operators, visits to Rajkot industrial area
