# Business Goals — Inika Resorts

## Primary Objective

Establish Inika Resorts as the go-to choice for discerning Bangalore travellers seeking a private, nature-immersive weekend retreat in Coorg. Build a sustainable direct booking channel that reduces reliance on OTAs (currently 60%+ OTA traffic) within 12 months.

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Current | Target | Timeline |
|-----|---------|--------|----------|
| Monthly organic bookings | ~4 | 12 | Q2 2026 |
| Direct bookings (website) | 35% | 55% | Q4 2026 |
| Google search ranking for primary keywords | Not in top 20 | Top 5 | Q3 2026 |
| GBP star rating | 4.1 | 4.5+ | Ongoing |
| Google reviews (quantity) | ~80 | 200+ | Q4 2026 |
| Organic sessions/month | ~300 | 1,500 | Q4 2026 |
| OTA commission savings | baseline | ₹2.5L/year | Q4 2026 |

## Strategic Priorities

1. **Own the "private resort Coorg" keyword space** — most competitors use generic "resort Coorg" without the privacy/luxury angle. This is Inika's differentiation and should be the primary SEO focus.
2. **Build a Google Business Profile that converts** — current GBP is under-optimised. Add more photos, respond to all reviews, seed Q&A with expert answers about the property.
3. **Create content that drives discovery searches** — blog posts targeting Bangalore-to-Coorg travel planning, romantic getaway keywords, weekend escape content.
4. **Reduce OTA dependency through price parity and direct incentives** — direct booking 10% discount or inclusive experience upgrade.
5. **Establish thought leadership on the "slow travel" and "intimate retreats" trend** — this aligns with both brand voice and growing search interest.
