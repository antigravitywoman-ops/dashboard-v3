# Brand Voice — Inika Resorts

## Voice & Tone

Warm, grounded, and unhurried. Inika Resorts speaks to guests as a trusted friend who has already discovered the escape they've been looking for. The voice is confident but never pushy — it doesn't oversell or use flashy superlatives. Instead, it paints vivid pictures of what guests will actually experience: cool mountain air, unhurried mornings, the sound of nothing but birds.

- **Adjectives**: Serene, genuine, unhurried, intimate, grounded, nature-immersive
- **Tone shift**: More personal and story-driven on social channels; more informative and practical on the website

## Audience

Primary: Urban professionals aged 28–45 seeking weekend escapes from Bangalore. They're well-traveled, discerning, and tired of overcrowded tourist spots. They value authenticity over polish, and genuine experiences over packaged entertainment.

## Language Guidelines

- **Sentence structure**: Short to medium. Vary length for rhythm. Never long or bureaucratic.
- **Reading level**: 7th–8th grade (accessible, not childish)
- **Active vs passive**: Prefer active voice. "We serve organic coffee from our own estate" not "Our organic coffee is served"
- **Jargon**: Avoid hospitality industry terms (room categories, meal plans). Describe the actual experience instead.
- **Numbers**: Spell out one through nine; use numerals for 10+. Use commas in thousands (1,500).
- **Punctuation**: Use em dashes sparingly for emphasis. Never use exclamation marks except in urgent operational messages.

## Words to Use

- hilltop, valley views, mountain air
- unhurried, peaceful, private
- curated, handpicked, designed for
- farm-to-table, organic, fresh
- genuine, real, authentic
- escape, retreat, sanctuary

## Words to Avoid

- luxurious, ultra-premium, world-class (generic and overused)
- amazing, incredible, breathtaking (cliché)
- state-of-the-art (irrelevant to guest experience)
- paradise (too abstract)
- tourist trap language ("must-see!")

## Writing Example

> Wake up to the sound of kingfishers. Your villa sits on a ridge overlooking a valley that stays green year-round — no manicured gardens, no noise, just the sound of the forest and a cup of coffee grown 800 metres above sea level.

## Social Media Voice

On Instagram and Reddit, the voice is slightly more conversational and story-driven. Share the real details — what time the mist settles in the morning, which path leads to the best sunset view, why the chef chooses a particular spice. Make the audience feel like they're already there.
