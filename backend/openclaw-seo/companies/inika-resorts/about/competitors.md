# Competitor Analysis — Inika Resorts

> Inika Resorts competes in the premium boutique resort segment for Bangalore-weekend-escape traffic in Coorg.

## Direct Competitor: The Tamara Coorg

**Website**: thetamara.com/coorg
**Strengths**:
- Established brand with strong OTA presence
- Wide range of room categories and price points
- Full-service spa and wellness centre
- Well-known for luxury positioning

**Weaknesses**:
- Larger property — less intimate feel
- Crowded on weekends due to high volume
- Premium pricing excludes mid-market
- Website content generic, lacks authentic storytelling

**Market Position**: Luxury mid-to-high price point. Appeals to the "proven" choice segment.

---

## Direct Competitor: Evolve Back Coorg

**Website**: evolveback.com/coorg
**Strengths**:
- Iconic heritage design — strong visual identity
- Excellent Google reviews (4.6+ stars)
- Multiple property locations in Coorg
- Strong social media presence

**Weaknesses**:
- Higher price point, not accessible to mid-market
- Focus on traditional luxury aesthetics, not intimate/nature-immersive
- Booking pages lack personality and conversion-focused copy
- SEO dominates for branded keywords — little organic non-brand presence

**Market Position**: Heritage luxury, appeals to wedding/honeymoon segment and corporate retreats.

---

## Indirect Competitor: Orange County Resort Coorg

**Website**: orangecounty.in/coorg
**Strengths**:
- Strong international tourist awareness
- Coffee plantation experience as differentiator
- Spacious layouts — good for families

**Weaknesses**:
- Dated website UX and SEO
- Very large property — no sense of intimacy
- Reviews mention declining service quality
- Content marketing non-existent

**Market Position**: Mid-range large property. Appeals to families and groups.

---

## Indirect Competitor: Discovery Village Coorg

**Website**: discoveryvillage.in
**Strengths**:
- Glamping/unique accommodation concept
- Strong Instagram presence
- Competitive pricing
- Focus on group bookings and corporates

**Weaknesses**:
- No SEO strategy — purely social traffic dependent
- Lower review ratings
- No differentiation beyond novelty accommodation
- Website doesn't support organic discovery

**Market Position**: Budget-unique glamping. Appeals to young travellers and bachelor parties.
