# Target Audience — Inika Resorts

## Persona 1: The Weekend Escape (Primary)

**Age/Role**: Couples, families, 28–55 years old, urban professionals
**Situation**: Based in Bangalore (2–4 hour drive), looking for a quick nature retreat away from city chaos. Wants comfortable accommodation with scenic views and privacy.
**Goals**: Relaxation, reconnecting with partner or family, escaping routine, experiencing nature without sacrificing comfort
**Pain Points**: Resorts too crowded on weekends, lack of privacy, overhyped locations, limited activities for kids, poor food quality
**Why Inika**: Private hilltop location overlooking valleys, curated couple-friendly experiences, outdoor activities in Coorg's nature, genuine reviews from similar visitors
**Discovery Path**: Instagram, Google ("resorts near Bangalore with private pool"), travel blogs, Tripadvisor, word of mouth

---
## Persona 2: The Special Occasion Planner

**Age/Role**: 30–50, planning a birthday, anniversary, or proposal
**Situation**: Wants a memorable, romantic setting that feels exclusive and well-managed. Not price-sensitive but expects premium service and attention to detail.
**Goals**: Create a truly memorable experience, beautiful photo opportunities, seamless coordination, privacy for the celebration
**Pain Points**: Standard hotel experience that feels impersonal, lack of customization, hidden charges, unresponsive staff
**Why Inika**: Bespoke celebration packages, outdoor setup for proposals/anniversaries, dedicated service, stunning natural backdrop that requires no decoration
**Discovery Path**: Google ("romantic resort Coorg proposal"), Instagram hashtags, wedding/celebration planning blogs, referral from past guests

---
## Persona 3: The Wellness Seeker (Secondary)

**Age/Role**: Solo travellers or couples, 25–45, health-conscious
**Situation**: Wants to combine nature with wellness — morning treks, plantation walks, organic food, stargazing, Ayurvedic spa options.
**Goals**: Digital detox, improved well-being, meaningful travel experiences, return to nature
**Pain Points**: Generic resort experiences, lack of genuine wellness offerings, overcrowding, no opportunity for solitude
**Why Inika**: Secluded hilltop property, nature-immersive activities, farm-to-table food, star-gazing terrace, walking trails
**Discovery Path**: Wellness travel blogs, Instagram nature/wellness accounts, sustainable travel platforms, Google ("eco resort Coorg")
