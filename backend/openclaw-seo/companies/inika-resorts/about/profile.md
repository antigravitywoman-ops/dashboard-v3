# Inika Resorts

**Founded**: ~2020s
**Headquarters**: Coorg, Karnataka, India
**Type**: Boutique Resort
**Target Market**: Weekend travellers from Bangalore seeking private, nature-immersive retreats

## Executive Summary

Inika Resorts is a boutique hilltop property offering private, nature-immersive stays in Coorg, Karnataka. Targeting Bangalore's discerning urban professionals (2–4 hour drive), Inika differentiates on privacy, curated experiences, and genuine nature immersion — positioning against crowded weekend resort options. Direct bookings are a primary growth target, with current 60%+ OTA dependency a key strategic challenge.

## Location

- **Property type**: Hilltop private retreat overlooking Coorg valleys
- **Target origin market**: Bangalore (primary), Chennai, Hyderabad (secondary)
- **Distance**: ~2–4 hour drive from Bangalore

## Value Proposition

Inika competes on intimacy and authenticity — not scale. The property's private hilltop location, curated couple-friendly experiences, outdoor activities, and farm-to-table food create a differentiated offering versus larger Coorg resorts.
