## Introduction

Known as the "Scotland of India," **Coorg** (Kodagu) is a breathtaking hill station in Karnataka that captivates visitors with its lush coffee plantations, misty mountains, and pristine waterfalls. Whether you're a nature lover, adventure seeker, or culture enthusiast, Coorg has something magical for everyone.

In this comprehensive guide, we at **Inika Resorts** share our favorite places to visit in Coorg, based on years of hosting guests from around the world.

## 1. Abbey Falls

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 8 km (20 minutes)

The iconic **Abbey Falls** is a must-visit destination in Coorg. Located amidst dense coffee plantations, this cascading waterfall drops from a height of about 70 feet.

- **Best Time to Visit**: October to March
- **Entry Fee**: ₹50 per person
- **Photography Tips**: Visit early morning for fewer crowds and best light

---

## 2. Raja's Seat

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 12 km (25 minutes)

Raja's Seat is a famous sunset point in Madikeri, offering panoramic views of the Coorg valley. According to local legend, the Kings of Kodagu used to spend evenings here enjoying the sunset with their queens.

- **Best Time to Visit**: Evening for sunset views
- **Entry Fee**: ₹20 per person
- **Tip**: Arrive 30 minutes before sunset for the best experience

---

## 3. Dubare Elephant Camp

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 15 km (35 minutes)

Experience elephants up close at **Dubare Elephant Camp**. You can bathe, feed, and interact with these gentle giants in their natural habitat.

- **Best Time to Visit**: November to May
- **Activities**: Elephant bathing, feeding, elephant ride
- **Booking**: Advance online booking recommended

---

## 4. Talakaveri

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 45 km (1.5 hours)

The source of the Kaveri River, **Talakaveri** is a sacred pilgrimage site located at Bhagamandala. The temple here is dedicated to Lord Kaveri.

- **Best Time to Visit**: October to March
- **Significance**: Origin of River Kaveri
- **Temple Timings**: 6:00 AM - 8:00 PM

---

## 5. Irupu Falls

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 50 km (1.5 hours)

The spectacular **Irupu Falls** is located on the borders of Coorg and North Kannada district. The falls are particularly majestic during monsoon season.

- **Best Time to Visit**: June to October (monsoon) or October to March (post-monsoon)
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Note**: Check weather conditions before visiting during monsoons

---

## 6. Namdroling Monastery

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 18 km (40 minutes)

Also known as the **Golden Temple**, Namdroling Monastery is one of the largest Tibetan settlements in South India. The stunning golden Buddha statues are a sight to behold.

- **Best Time to Visit**: Year-round
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Tips**: Dress modestly and maintain silence

---

## 7. Nisargadhama

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 20 km (45 minutes)

A beautiful island formed by the Kaveri River, **Nisargadhama** is a paradise for nature lovers. It features a deer park, Buddhist temple, and river-side cottage.

- **Best Time to Visit**: October to March
- **Entry Fee**: ₹50 per person
- **Activities**: Boating, nature walk, deer park

---

## 8. Brahmagiri Peak

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 55 km (2 hours)

For adventure enthusiasts, **Brahmagiri Peak** offers excellent trekking opportunities. The peak is part of the Western Ghats and offers breathtaking views.

- **Best Time to Visit**: October to May
- **Trek Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Permit**: Required from forest department

## 9. Mandalpatti Viewpoint

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 25 km (1 hour)

Also known as **Mullugundi Viewpoint**, Mandalpatti offers stunning 360-degree views of the Coorg valley. The viewpoint is especially popular for sunrise views.

- **Best Time to Visit**: Sunrise or sunset
- **Entry Fee**: ₹30 per person (jeep hire extra)
- **Tip**: Hire a jeep from Kushalnagar for the rough terrain

## 10. Omkareshwara Temple

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 12 km (25 minutes)

A unique temple in Madikeri, **Omkareshwara Temple** features a blend of Islamic and Gothic architecture. The temple houses a sacred lingam believed to have been installed by Lord Rama.

- **Best Time to Visit**: Year-round
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Architecture**: Indo-Saracenic style

---

## 11. Kote Betta Temple

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 12 km (25 minutes)

Perched atop a hill in Madikeri, the **Kote Betta Temple** offers panoramic views of the town. It's dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati.

- **Best Time to Visit**: Early morning or evening
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Temple Timings**: 6:00 AM - 8:00 PM

## 13. Cauvery Nisargadhama

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 20 km (45 minutes)

Another beautiful spot on the Kaveri River, **Cauvery Nisargadhama** features boating facilities and a bamboo grove.

- **Best Time to Visit**: October to March

## 14. Bagamandala

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 35 km (1 hour)

Famous for the Triveni Sangama (confluence of three rivers), **Bagamandala** is a pilgrimage site where the Kaveri, Cauvery, and Kannike rivers meet.

- **Best Time to Visit**: October to March
- **Significance**: Pilgrimage site
- **Temple**: Bhagandeshwara Temple

---

## 15. Chelavara Falls

**Distance from Inika Resorts**: 30 km (1 hour)

A hidden gem, **Chelavara Falls** is less crowded than Abbey Falls but equally beautiful. The falls are best visited during monsoon season.

- **Best Time to Visit**: June to October
- **Entry Fee**: Free
- **Tip**: Wear comfortable shoes for the trek
